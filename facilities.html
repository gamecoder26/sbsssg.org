Our Facilities

The School imparts education in Pre-primary, Primary, Junior, High school & Intermediate,UG,PG,BTC,B.Ed. sections with the aid to latest audio-visual, psychological & scientific apparatus.


The institution nurtures social equality, cultural values & nationalism in children.


Our goal is to help the children grow up in the best possible environment to develop a sense of observation, to generate healthy habits and ensure emotional integration in them


All the rooms of the multi-storeyed decent buildings of our school are spacious, airy, well-electrified, fully-fumished and well separated form each other.


Due care is taken to maintain balance in number of students and strength of teaching staff so that the teachers may be able to handle the students efficiently and individual attention can be paid for a good all-pound personality development.


In the school, House system has been made for all Primary, Junior, High School & Intermediate classes for an overall development of the students not only in educational field but in the other extra-curricular activities also. In the matter of discipline House system has been a helping hand to the administration. The Inter House competitions are conducted from time to and Winner House is awarded by the expert judges.


Compulsory computer education is imparted to the students from Ist to VIIIth standard. Computer Science is an optional subject in High School & Intermediate classes.


A full fledged inspection department ensures that teaching standard and systematically prepared lesson plans are strictly maintained by the teachers.


Various magazines and books written by eminent personalities and authorities have been introduced in school library to keep the teachers well equipped with present day information and knowledge.


Each and every child is attended individually. Daily homework is checked carefully. Monthly tests are held and suggestions for childs further progress are regularly given to the parents.
